How do I colour in a picture?

Tap Colour a Picture, choose a category along the top and pick a picture. Choose the paint tin and tap any area to fill it with colour right up to the lines. The black outlines always stay on top, and the eraser never rubs them out.

How do I draw straight lines?

Tap the ruler button at the top of the page. Drag the ruler with one finger, or turn it with two. It clicks into place at straight and diagonal angles. Draw next to its edge and the line comes out perfectly straight. Tap the button again to put the ruler away.

How do I undo a mistake?

Tap the curved arrow at the top, or tap the page with two fingers. Tap with three fingers (or the other arrow) to redo.

How do I zoom in?

Pinch with two fingers to zoom in, and drag with two fingers to move around the page.

Where are my drawings saved?

Everything saves by itself as you draw. Find your pictures in My Drawings on the home screen. Touch and hold a drawing to copy or delete it.

How do I print?

Tap the printer button at the top of the page. To print a blank colouring page to colour with crayons, touch and hold a picture in the gallery and choose Print Colouring Page.

How do I turn sounds on?

Tap the small gear at the top of the home screen and answer the grown-up question. Sounds are off to start with; haptics are on.
