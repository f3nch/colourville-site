Colourville

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Colourville is a colouring and drawing app for children. It is designed to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Colourville does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your drawings and coloured-in pictures, with any words added to them;
  • the colours used most recently;
  • your settings: sounds and haptics.

This information never leaves the device. We can't see it and have no way to access it. If the device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include it, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Internet access

The app makes no network connections and contains no links to websites or other apps.

Printing

Printing uses the print service built into iOS and iPadOS. Pictures go straight from your device to your printer; we never receive them.

Children

The app is made for children to use. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing for a parent to request or delete from us. The grown-ups settings screen sits behind a parental check.

Deleting your data

To delete a drawing, touch and hold it in My Drawings and choose Delete. To remove everything, delete the app from the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
